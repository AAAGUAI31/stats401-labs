How to use the interaction

Interaction guide

  1. Click a region or development legend item to select it; click the selected item again to clear that filter.
  2. Press Start voice control, allow microphone access, and speak one region command.
  3. Press Start camera, allow camera access, and wait until the MediaPipe model is ready.
  4. Show Thumb Down to move from All → Low → Medium → High. Show Thumb Up to move from All → High → Medium → Low.
  5. When both a development level and a region are selected, only cities matching both remain prominent.

Design reflection

Why these interactions fit the task

This project extends the multivariate city scatterplot from Lab 2. The original visualization maps population to horizontal position, average temperature to vertical position, region to color, and development level to circle size. I added two novel input modalities. Voice commands such as “Show North” highlight a geographic region, while Thumb Down moves to a larger development level and Thumb Up moves to a smaller level. When both controls are active, only cities matching both selections remain prominent, which supports focused comparison without removing context. Visible status messages show the detected command, gesture, and resulting selection. The current-selection summary keeps both filters and the match count visible beside the chart. The chart legends also act as direct filters, which provides a precise alternative when recognition is unavailable; clicking an active legend item again clears that dimension. These mappings are easy to remember because speech names the desired region directly and the gestures move through an ordered size scale. Limitations include browser-dependent speech recognition (sometime the speech recognition fails to distingish speech, especially for show south, which is easily be recognized as show sauce or show source), camera permissions, hand orientation, and occasional gesture misclassification. A cooldown prevents a held gesture from changing several levels at once.
